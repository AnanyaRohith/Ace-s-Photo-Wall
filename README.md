# ace's photo wall ✶

*A little corner of the internet for photos, polaroids, scribbles and things that deserve a spot on the wall.*

A digital photo wall, except you can actually interact with it.

Pin things up. Move them around. Write little notes. Draw on the wall. Take things down and decide what happens to them.

It's a photo wall. It just has a few more tricks.

---

## ✶ the wall

![Ace's Photo Wall](assets/photo-wall.png)

## what can you do here?

**01 — Polaroids**

Add photos and arrange them on the wall. Choose between different sizes, move them around and flip them over to reveal the other side.

**02 — Magnets**

Pin your Polaroids in place with little star magnets. Double-tap a magnet to take it down.

**03 — Notes**

Leave little notes on the wall. When you're done with one, watch it crumple up and find its way into the bin.

**04 — The Keepsake Jar**

Not everything needs to stay on the wall. Move things into the jar and bring them back whenever you feel like it.

**05 — The Dustbin**

A place for things you've decided to discard. Retrieve items or empty the bin when you're ready.

**06 — A little customisation**

Change the heading, play around with typography, draw directly on the wall and rearrange things until they feel right.

## little details

- Three Polaroid sizes.
- Drag-and-drop positioning.
- Editable wall heading and typography.
- Freehand drawing with adjustable brush settings.
- Foldable, movable and dockable toolbar.
- Photo mode for a cleaner, distraction-free view.
- Custom star cursor.
- Local browser storage to preserve your wall between visits.

---

## under the hood

Built using:

- **HTML** — structure
- **CSS** — styling, layouts, animations and visual details
- **JavaScript** — interactions and functionality
- **localStorage** — saving the wall in the browser

No framework. No backend. Just the browser doing its thing.

Your wall is stored locally in your browser, so it isn't automatically synced across devices.
